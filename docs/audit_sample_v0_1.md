## Inclusion rule

1. Product must have non-suspended marketing authorisation status.
2. Product must be parenteral according to the pre-specified product-name filter.
3. Famotidine (A02BA03): include all eligible unique licences.
4. Omeprazole (A02BC01), pantoprazole (A02BC02), esomeprazole (A02BC05):
   include the oldest and newest eligible unique licence by marketing-authorisation date.
5. Selection is independent of packaging route, manufacturing site,
   document availability, or expected network topology.
